<div class="report-intro">
  <p>A series of policy reports produced through a joint project between the University of Washington
  and the <a href="https://kcrha.org/">King County Regional Homelessness Authority</a>, analyzing the
  biennial Point-in-Time survey to understand unsheltered homelessness. The series draws on
  respondent-driven sampling surveys conducted from 2022 to 2026.</p>
  <p class="report-meta">The full set of reports is published by the
  <a href="https://csde.washington.edu/research/population-research-and-policy/">UW Center for Studies
  in Demography and Ecology</a>.</p>
</div>
